# The dashboard

The management dashboard is where an organization is run: projects and secrets,
who can see what, machine credentials, audit history, and billing. Click any
screenshot to enlarge it.

## Signing in and registering

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/login.png" alt="Kryptic sign-in page" />

Sign in with email and password, or with any SSO provider your organization has
enabled. Those buttons appear only for providers that are actually configured.
If the email domain has SAML, a one-click SSO button appears after you type the
address.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/register.png" alt="Create your organization" />

**Register** creates a new organization. You become the Owner. A `?plan=team`
or `?plan=business` link starts a 14-day trial with no card. Invite the rest of
the team after you are in.

## Chrome, roles, and the vault

The sidebar is split into **Workspace** (Dashboard, Projects, Secret scanning)
and **Organization** (Members, Groups, Machine identities, Daemon sessions,
Approvals, Audit log, Integrations, Settings). Owners and Admins see the full
Organization block. Everyone else sees **My daemons** instead of that block.

The top bar has jump-to-project search, a notifications bell, and the theme
toggle.

Roles are Owner, Admin, Developer, and Viewer. There are no custom roles.

Reveal, import, export, creating a machine identity, and sealing the
organization key all need the vault unlocked. Enroll, unlock, and rotate under
**Settings → Encryption**. The passphrase never leaves the browser.

## Overview

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/dashboard.png" alt="Kryptic dashboard" />

The dashboard is the operational summary: member, project, secret and active
daemon counts; a live activity monitor (your own rows unless you are Admin+);
trials and invitations that are about to expire; an Approvals summary that
links to the inbox; group and machine-identity summaries; and a CI client
generator for GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps, Docker, Compose,
dotenv, and the other runners listed in [CI/CD](/ci). Prefer
[`kryptic ci export`](/cli#ci-export) in those snippets: the
platform serves ciphertext, and the CLI decrypts on the runner.

If this browser saw a daemon device-flow code that is still waiting, a banner
at the top links to **Approve daemon** (`/device`).

## Projects

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/projects.png" alt="Projects list" />

A project maps to a repository. Creating one auto-creates the `development`,
`staging` and `production` environments and shows the `kryptic.json` to commit.
Developers and Viewers can **Request access** from this page (environments,
level, and a reason). Admins approve those requests on [Approvals](#approvals).

### Secrets

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/project-secrets.png" alt="Secrets matrix" />

Rows are keys, columns are environments. **Values are never sent to the browser
by default**. Cells are masked, and revealing one is an explicit, audit-logged
call that decrypts in the browser under the org key.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/project-secret-revealed.png" alt="A revealed secret" />

Each cell offers reveal, copy, version history with restore, and delete. The
toolbar handles `.env` import and per-environment export. Import encrypts in
the browser before upload.

### Client generator

The **Client generator** tab emits a ready-to-paste snippet for the project's
id and a chosen environment: GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps, Docker,
Compose, dotenv, and the rest of the [CI/CD](/ci) catalogue. Store the machine
identity's client id and secret in the runner's secret store, paste, done.

### Environments

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/project-environments.png" alt="Environments tab" />

Add custom environments such as `qa` or `preview` within your plan's limit. You
cannot delete the last environment, and deleting one cleans up its values.
Admin only.

### Access

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/project-access.png" alt="Project access tab" />

Grants tie a user *or a group* to this project with a level **per environment**.
This is project access, not the organization-key grant (that lives on
Approvals). See [Access control](/access-control) for how grants and roles
combine. Admin only.

### Settings

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/project-settings.png" alt="Project settings tab" />

Rename the project, copy its `kryptic.json`, or delete it. Deletion requires
typing the project name. Admin only.

## Members

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/members.png" alt="Members" />

Invite several emails at once (one per line or comma-separated), assign a
built-in role (Admin, Developer, or Viewer), deactivate or reactivate people,
and revoke sessions. There is no CSV import and no custom roles. Pending
invitations can be resent (which issues a fresh token and invalidates the old
one) or cancelled. Invitees accept at `/invitations/accept` by setting a name
and a password.

## Groups

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/groups.png" alt="Groups" />

Groups hold project access grants. Granting "Backend Team" access to a project
survives staff changes: you manage membership, not a dozen individual grants.

## Machine identities

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/machine-identities.png" alt="Machine identities" />

Credentials for CI/CD and other non-human callers. Unlock the vault to create
one: the browser generates the key pair and can seal the organization key.
The client secret is shown **exactly once** at creation and stored only as an
Argon2id hash. There is no project-scoping control in the dashboard. Rotate or
deactivate from this page. Pending machine grants, if any, are approved on
**Approvals**. See [Machine identities](/machine-identities).

## Daemon sessions and device approval

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/daemon-sessions.png" alt="Daemon sessions" />

Every signed-in developer machine: device, platform, version, IP and last seen.
Admins see the whole organization and can revoke any session. This is the
"someone lost their laptop" control. Non-admins see only their own devices
(**My daemons**).

Approving a device-flow code on `/device` binds the daemon to the account.
Decrypt still needs an organization-key grant. An admin can grant that key on
the device page after approval, or later on Approvals. A signed-in daemon
without a grant cannot serve a single secret.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/device-approve.png" alt="Approve a daemon" />

## Approvals

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/approvals.png" alt="Approvals inbox" />

**Approvals** is the admin inbox for two different queues:

1. **Organization-key grants** (org-wide). Members, devices, and CI machines
   wait here until an admin seals the org key. The vault must be unlocked.
2. **Project access requests** (per project and environment). Independent of
   the org-key grant. Approving creates the grant.

Encryption setup stays under **Settings → Encryption**. Grants are not approved
there.

## Audit log

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/audit-log.png" alt="Audit log" />

Every mutation and every secret read, filterable by action, target type and date
range, exportable to CSV. The rule is **audit the action, never the value**: a
reveal entry names the key and environment, never the secret.

## Secret scanning

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/secret-scanning.png" alt="Secret scanning" />

Paste file content or a git patch and scan it against 222 gitleaks rules. The
same engine runs fully offline in the CLI. See [`kryptic scan`](/cli#secret-scanning).

## Integrations

OAuth, SAML and SCIM live under **Integrations**, not Settings.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/integrations-sso.png" alt="Single sign-on" />

- **Single sign-on** - connect the organization's own OAuth apps (Google,
  Microsoft, GitHub, GitLab) and claim an email domain for auto-provisioning.
- **Directory import** - pull users and groups from Microsoft Entra (and Google
  Workspace when configured).
- **SAML 2.0** - Okta, JumpCloud, or a generic SAML 2.0 provider. Connecting
  Okta issues a SCIM token in the same step. See [Set up Okta](/enterprise/okta).
- **Provisioning** - SCIM 2.0 token issue and revoke. See [SCIM](/enterprise/scim).

The stored OAuth client secret is encrypted with the organization's operational
data key, not the client-held org key.

## Settings

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/settings-general.png" alt="Organization settings" />

**General** holds the organization profile, billing email, slug, and the
session expiry policy that caps how long any login stays valid.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/settings-encryption.png" alt="Encryption settings" />

**Encryption** is where an Owner or Admin enrolls a vault, initializes the org
key, downloads the Emergency Kit, unlocks or locks the vault, recovers with the
recovery code, and rotates the key. Every cryptographic operation runs in the
browser. Pending grants are approved on [Approvals](#approvals), not here. See
[Encryption](/encryption).

**Billing details** is the legal invoice record: company name, address, country,
and VAT. Greek VAT numbers are resolved from the AADE registry.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/settings-billing-details.png" alt="Billing details" />

### Plans and billing

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/settings-billing.png" alt="Plans and billing" />

**Plans & billing** is plan and seat usage, 14-day trials with no card, Stripe
checkout (monthly or annual, VAT handled), and seat adjustment with proration.

**Invoices** lists every tax invoice as a downloadable PDF.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/settings-invoices.png" alt="Invoices" />

**Danger zone** (Owner only) deletes the organization and everything in it.
You type the organization name and your password. There is no undo.

<img className="imagewrapper-big" src="/screenshots/settings-danger-zone.png" alt="Danger zone" />

<Note>
  Self-hosted deployments without Stripe configured show a sales pointer instead
  of a broken checkout. Nothing in the dashboard depends on billing being set up.
</Note>
